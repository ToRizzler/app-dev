# Sword Art Online (SAO)

Sword Art Online is a Japanese light novel and anime series written by Reki Kawahara. Set in the near future, the story follows players trapped inside a Virtual Reality Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Game (**VRMMORPG**) using a full-dive helmet called the NerveGear.

> "This might be a game, but it's not something you play." — *Akihiko Kayaba*

---

## Story Overview

In November 2022, 10,000 players log into Sword Art Online for its official launch, only to discover that the Log Out button has been removed. To escape the floating castle of Aincrad, players must clear all 100 floors because dying in the game means dying in the real world.

### Main Characters
- **Kirito (Kazuto Kirigaya)** – A solo beta tester known as the *Black Swordsman*
- **Asuna Yuuki** – Sub-leader of the *Knights of the Blood Oath* guild
- **Klein (Ryotaro Tsuboi)** – Kirito's first friend in Aincrad and leader of *Fuurinkazan*
- **Sinon (Shino Asada)** – A skilled sniper introduced in the *Gun Gale Online* arc
